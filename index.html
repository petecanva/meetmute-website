The problem

You're in two meetings. Both are talking at you.

Modern work means juggling multiple calls — a client meeting, a team standup, an all-hands — all running simultaneously in separate tabs.

Google Meet gives you a mic mute button. But there's no speaker mute. You either hear everything or you leave.

MeetMute adds the missing control: silence any meeting's audio output without leaving, without disrupting, and without touching your system volume.
